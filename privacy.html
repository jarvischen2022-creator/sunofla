Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Sunofla collects and uses information when you visit this website, request Pilot Access, or request onsite service.

Information you provide

When you request Pilot Access, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, a high-level description of your workflow, the AI tools you currently use, and your preferred time to talk.

When you request onsite service, we may collect your business name, contact name, email address, phone number, service address, service selections, scheduling preference, and a description of the issue.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, prepare an appropriate scope or estimate, schedule service, communicate about an active Pilot or onsite engagement, and improve how we explain and deliver our services.

What not to submit

Please do not submit passwords, access codes, payment card details, government identification numbers, client files, patient information, or other confidential information through website forms.

Service providers and AI tools

Sunofla may use service providers to operate this website, deliver form notifications, host a Pilot workspace, or provide AI and cloud services selected for a specific engagement. The appropriate deployment approach — cloud, local, or hybrid — is discussed before a Pilot or project begins.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information submitted through this website. We share information only with service providers needed to operate the website, respond to your request, deliver an agreed service, or when required by law.

We keep inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide services, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete the contact information you submitted, subject to records we may need to keep for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact:
