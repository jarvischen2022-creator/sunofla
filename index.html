COST CONTROL

What is AI actually costing the business?

AI spending is more than a subscription. It includes model usage, unnecessary premium requests, repeated work, and the time people spend checking results.

The Workspace makes those choices visible by workflow, routing routine work to cost-efficient AI and showing where higher-cost AI is justified.
