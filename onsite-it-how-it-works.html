WHAT WE DISCUSS FIRST

Begin with the work your team already repeats.

The conversation centers on one practical task and the people, information, and tools around it.

THE WORKFLOW

What does the team repeat today?

A client update, proposal draft, project intake, internal search, document review, or another task that takes time and creates follow-up work.

FILES AND SOURCES

What information should be approved?

We identify the folders, templates, procedures, and current tools that may be relevant — without asking you to upload confidential files.

PEOPLE AND BOUNDARIES

Who needs access, review, or approval?

We discuss roles, sensitive information, existing AI use, and where a human must remain responsible for the final decision.

PRACTICAL CONSTRAINTS

What is already working — and what is getting in the way?

We consider current cloud tools, shared files, network conditions, storage, backup, cost concerns, and the team's available time.

A PRACTICAL FIRST CONVERSATION

A clear conversation before a larger decision.

The first conversation helps clarify the workflow, the boundaries that matter, and the most reasonable next step. It gives both sides enough context to decide whether deeper work is useful.

The discovery conversation

  • Understand one workflow at a high level
  • Identify the main files, roles, boundaries, and concerns
  • Discuss whether a Pilot may be a practical fit
  • Identify obvious cloud, local, or IT-readiness considerations
  • Recommend the most practical next conversation or next step

If we continue

A Pilot or implementation project can define the detailed workflow, rules, permissions, review points, AI routes, testing, documentation, equipment, or onsite work needed for that specific scope.

Any Pilot or implementation work is scoped and approved in writing before it begins.

POSSIBLE NEXT STEPS

Choose the next step that fits the work.

A useful conversation does not always lead to the same answer. The right next step depends on the workflow, the team's boundaries, and the current environment.

01

Keep current tools for now

The team may already have what it needs. A clearer workflow, better habits, or a later review may be more useful than adding anything new today.

02

Prepare the environment

Shared files, access rules, backup, or an existing network issue may need attention before a controlled workflow can be tested.

03

Run a guided Pilot

Define approved sources, user roles, AI routes, human review points, and the evidence to review with a small group using real work.

04

Plan implementation
