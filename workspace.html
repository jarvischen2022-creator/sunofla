Permissions

Everyone sees only what their role allows.

Set access once. The Sunofla AI Workspace checks every request against it — client files stay with the client team, and HR files stay with HR.

No blanket access, no guessing. When someone asks for something outside their role, the request stops before it starts.

Data protection

Sensitive fields get caught before they travel.

Configured rules scan each request. Prohibited data is stopped outright; protected fields can be flagged or redacted before the request continues.

These are practical data-protection controls — visible in the interface, adjustable to your policy, and recorded every time.

Cost control

The right AI route for each task — with the cost shown.

Policy and task type choose the approved option. Work that must stay in your approved environment runs locally; routine work takes an efficient route; premium capacity only when it’s justified.

Every choice and its cost is visible before the task runs — and recorded after.

Human review

A person approves what matters. Everything is recorded.

Important output stays a draft until someone reviews it. The record shows the sources used, the route taken, the reviewer, and what it cost.

AI prepares the work. Your team remains responsible for the decision — with the evidence to show it.
