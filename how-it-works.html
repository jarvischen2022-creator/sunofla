From first visit to ongoing support

A practical path with a decision at every stage.

The process starts with the interruption, workflow, data, access, and risk. Hardware follows only when the assessment shows that local compute, shared storage, network upgrades, or continuity infrastructure are the right answer.

01

Workflow & Systems Assessment

Map the problem, tools, data, access, and risk.

We inventory the systems involved, identify the bottleneck, review access and recovery needs, and define the smallest useful next step.

02

AI Workflow Pilot

Prove one workflow with a small team.

Run one real workflow with clear sources, roles, review points, and a defined outcome. Expand only after the team can review the evidence.

03

Shared AI Workspace

Give the team one governed workspace.

When collaboration requires it, we add shared files, permissions, NAS or backup, and a team AI workspace around approved information.

04

Private AI & Business Continuity

Build a system for teams that cannot afford downtime.

Dedicated AI compute, high-speed storage, backup copies, UPS, monitoring, and recovery practices are designed to the real workload.

05

Managed AI & IT Support

Keep access, backups, and workflows healthy.

Monthly support can cover backup verification, account access, template improvements, updates, and quarterly onsite reviews.

The first decision

Find out what should be fixed, piloted, or left alone.

An assessment does not assume you need a GPU PC, NAS, or private system. It identifies the smallest useful next step and the controls needed to use it responsibly.
