The design principle

PCs think and do. Storage protects and shares.

A GPU workstation handles local AI compute. A NAS does a different job: it gives the team a durable, permissioned, recoverable source of files.

  • AI PC: models, retrieval, transcription, and automation
  • NAS: project files, permissions, snapshots, and sharing
  • Cloud: complex or occasional tasks that need current large models

Workflow packs

Make one daily workflow work first.

No generic prompt course. Each pilot defines the input, a repeatable template, the person who reviews it, and where the approved result belongs.

01

Project intake & brief

Turn meeting notes, client messages, and a project brief into a summary, action list, and risks.

Control:

The project lead reviews before anything is saved. AI does not contact the client.

02

Proposal draft

Use a fixed intake form to create a first draft and a list of questions about scope, timing, and assumptions.

Control:

A responsible person verifies pricing, commitments, and terms before sending.

03

Internal knowledge search

Search only approved SOPs, product information, and prior project templates.

Control:

Answers cite the source file or location and send uncertain items to a person.

Safe by design

AI gets boundaries, not blanket access.

Agents begin with read-only access to explicitly approved information. Writing files, sending messages, changing orders, or publishing content requires human confirmation. Fully offline is an option only when it fits the actual risk and workflow.
