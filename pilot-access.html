What happens after you request

Four steps, no surprises.

1. Choose a workflow

We pick one repeated, document-heavy task your team already does — the pilot starts there, not with a company-wide rollout.

2. Set the rules

Together we configure who can access what, which sources are approved, and what needs a human review.

3. Run the pilot

Your team uses Sunofla AI Workspace on real work for 2–4 weeks, with permissions, data-protection checks, and visible AI spending from day one.

4. Review the evidence

You see what was controlled, what it cost, and what changed — then decide whether to expand, with the numbers in front of you.
